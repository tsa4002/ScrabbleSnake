Guide

  • Build an inventory of letters to spell out the challenge words before the timer runs out!
  • Words score the sum of their Scrabble letter values. To ensure appropriateness of challenge words, they are sourced here.
  • In this version, running into your own body makes you lose your tiles.

Controls

  • Move with the arrow keys. Press Enter to submit.
  • Tab to toggle between text input box and arena.
